01

Brand Essence

HomeQuarters is where the global African diaspora finds its table. Not a platform. Not an app. A community that holds space for people who have built lives across borders — and deserve a room of their own in every city.

01 — Belonging
You are already home.

Every touchpoint — from onboarding to the moment a member walks into a partner venue — should feel like being welcomed, not processed. HQ removes the friction of being a newcomer.

02 — Discernment
Curated, never crowded.

Membership is by invitation only. Partners are selected, not solicited. Every recommendation carries the weight of genuine consideration. We do not optimise for volume.

03 — Warmth
Luxury that is human.

HomeQuarters sits between the polish of a private members' club and the ease of a friend's recommendation. Elevated, never cold. Considered, never formal.

Clear space

Always maintain clear space equal to the cap-height of the 'H' on all sides of the wordmark. Do not crowd the logo with other elements.

Minimum size

Digital: no smaller than 120px wide. Print: no smaller than 30mm wide. Below these thresholds use the monogram mark instead.

Approved colours

Gold on Dark. Dark on Cream. Dark on Gold. White on Dark in exceptional circumstances only. Never apply a drop shadow or glow effect.

The HQ mark

The monogram is distilled from the wordmark — Cormorant Garamond SemiBold, tracked at +8. It is the compact identity of HomeQuarters: used where the full wordmark cannot breathe, where space demands brevity, where recognition must work at a glance.

The rounded square format (corner radius 22.5%) is reserved for the app icon only. All other uses of the monogram appear as letterforms only, without a containing shape.

Gold is a privilege, not a default

Reserve gold for the most significant moments: membership tier indicators, calls to action, the wordmark on dark. When everything is gold, nothing is. Used with restraint it commands attention; used freely it becomes noise.

Dark and Cream do the work

The primary palette is intentionally limited. Dark backgrounds project authority and exclusivity. Cream provides warmth and legibility for long-form content. Together they carry 90% of the visual language — gold completes it.

06

Voice & Tone

Write like this

Approval
Your application has been approved. Welcome to HomeQuarters.
Redemption
Your benefit has been redeemed. The team at Rivington knows to expect you.
Rejection
We're not able to offer membership at this time. You're welcome to apply again in six months.

Not like this

Too excitable
Congrats!! You're officially IN! 🎉 Welcome to the HQ fam!
Too corporate
Your redemption transaction has been successfully processed. Reference: TXN-00291.
Too cold
Application rejected. You do not meet current criteria.

Warm, not effusive

We speak with the ease of a trusted friend, not the enthusiasm of a brand. Say the thing simply. A warm sentence lands harder than three excited ones.

Precise, not cold

Clarity is a form of respect. Be specific — name the venue, name the benefit, name the person. Never hide behind generic copy when the real thing is available.

Confident, not boastful

HQ does not need to tell people it is exclusive. The experience communicates it. Write with the quiet authority of something that does not need to shout.

07

Photography & Imagery

Light & Atmosphere

Warm, low ambient light. Golden hour in dining rooms. Candle-lit tables. Rich shadows. The visual texture of a place worth being. No bright studio flash. No artificial fill that flattens depth.

People

Diaspora represented naturally — in conversation, laughing, leaning in — never posed, never performing for the camera. Real moments of connection, never stock archetypes. Always dignified.

Food & Venues

Food as context, not subject. Shoot a table mid-meal, not a styled dish on white. Show the room, not just the plate. The venue should feel lived-in and worth seeking out.

Do

  • Natural light or warm artificial light
  • Authentic candid moments between people
  • Rich, saturated colour grading — deep shadows
  • African diaspora represented as protagonists, not tokens
  • Editorial framing: wide shots that establish place
  • Imperfect, lived-in environments

Don't

  • Stock photography or generic lifestyle imagery
  • Over-lit, flat, bright white backgrounds
  • Food photography divorced from its context
  • Tokenistic or exoticising representation
  • Filters or effects that feel Instagram-generic
  • Logos or watermarks other than the HQ monogram
08

Logo Misuse

HomeQuarters

No shadows or effects

Drop shadows, glows, and gradients on the wordmark are never permitted. The logo needs no embellishment.

HomeQuarters

No unauthorised colours

The wordmark must only appear in Gold, Dark, Cream, or pure White. Never in blue, red, or any other colour.

HomeQuarters

No stretching or distorting

Never scale the wordmark non-uniformly. The letterform proportions of Cormorant Garamond are fixed and should never be altered.

HomeQuarters

No font substitutions

The wordmark is always set in Cormorant Garamond SemiBold. Georgia, Times New Roman, and other serif substitutes are not acceptable.

HomeQuarters

No unapproved backgrounds

Do not place the wordmark on colourful, patterned, or photographic backgrounds unless Gold or Dark contrast can be guaranteed.

HomeQuarters

No low-opacity treatments

Do not use the wordmark as a watermark or background texture. If legibility cannot be maintained, use the monogram instead.
