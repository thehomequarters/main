Privacy Policy

Last updated March 2026

1. Who We Are

HomeQuarters ("HQ", "we", "us") is a private members' community operated by HomeQuarters Ltd, registered in England and Wales. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our mobile application.

2. Who Can Use HQ

HomeQuarters is open to adults aged 21 and over only. We do not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 21. If you believe a user under 21 has registered, please contact us at hello@thehomequarters.com and we will take immediate action.

3. Data We Collect

We collect the following information when you apply for and use a membership: • Identity data: first name, last name • Contact data: email address, phone number (optional) • Profile data: title, bio, city, industry, interests, Instagram handle, LinkedIn handle, profile photo • Membership data: member code, membership tier, membership status, invitation code used • Device data: push notification token (if permission granted) • Usage data: deal redemption history, event bookings, connection activity • Payment data: subscription tier and status (payment card details are handled entirely by Stripe and are never stored by HQ) All data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore and Storage).

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to: • Operate and maintain your membership account • Display your profile to other active members (subject to your privacy settings) • Process subscription payments via Stripe • Send push notifications about messages, connection requests, and membership updates • Facilitate event bookings and deal redemptions • Communicate with you about your membership • Maintain the safety and integrity of the community We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Your Privacy Controls

You control what other members can see about you. From Account > Privacy you can: • Hide your city from other members • Hide your industry from other members • Hide your interests from other members • Hide your Instagram and LinkedIn links • Restrict direct messages to connections only Your name and profile photo are always visible to active members as part of the community experience.

6. Third-Party Services

We share limited data with the following trusted third parties: • Google Firebase - secure storage of all profile and account data • Stripe - payment processing (card details never touch our servers) • Airalo - eSIM fulfilment for Platinum members who opt in • OpenStreetMap - anonymous venue map requests • Unsplash - venue imagery CDN Each provider operates under its own privacy policy and data processing agreements.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your membership is active. If you cancel your membership or your account is terminated, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, except where we are required by law to retain it for longer (e.g. billing records, which we retain for 7 years under UK tax law).

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: • Access the personal data we hold about you • Request correction of inaccurate data • Request deletion of your data (right to erasure) • Object to or restrict certain processing • Data portability To exercise any of these rights, email hello@thehomequarters.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS for all data in transit and Firebase security rules to restrict access. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

10. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, we store your device push token in Firestore to send you alerts about messages, connection requests, and membership updates. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

11. Content Policy

HomeQuarters is not an adult-content platform. All content shared within the app must be appropriate for a professional social setting. No sexually explicit material, graphic violence, or illegal content is permitted. Violations result in immediate account termination.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you via the app when we make material changes. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact: HomeQuarters Ltd hello@thehomequarters.com
