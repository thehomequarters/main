Life at HomeQuarters
The Code We Live By

House Rules.

HomeQuarters is more than an app - it's a community. These rules exist to protect the culture we're building together.

✦

Friendship first, always.

HQ is not a networking platform. Do not cold-message members for jobs, business opportunities, or unsolicited pitches. We are here because of a shared passion for our homelands - let genuine friendship be the foundation of every connection.

01

Respect Every Member

Treat every member, guest, and venue staff with courtesy and dignity. Discrimination, harassment, or intimidation of any kind will not be tolerated.

02

Honour the Space

Our partner venues open their doors to us - treat them as you would your own home. Leave spaces as you found them, respect venue rules, and tip generously.

03

Keep It Confidential

What happens at HQ stays at HQ. Do not share other members' personal information, conversations, or photos without their explicit consent.

04

Redeem Responsibly

Member benefits are for personal use only. Do not abuse, resell, or share your QR codes. One redemption per benefit per visit unless stated otherwise.

05

Represent the Culture

As an HQ member, you represent the community. Whether at events or partner venues, carry yourself with pride and embody the values of the diaspora.

06

Membership Can Be Revoked

Violation of these rules may result in immediate suspension or permanent revocation - regardless of tier. Access is a privilege upheld by your conduct, not a right.

These rules apply equally to all membership tiers. Last updated March 2026.

Membership

HomeQuarters is a private members' club for the diaspora. We curate access to the best venues, restaurants, and events in the UK — tailored to specific diaspora communities — and give members a place to connect with people who share their roots.

HomeQuarters is built to serve multiple diaspora communities. Our first is Zimbabweans in the UK, with more communities launching soon. Each community gets its own curated set of venues, events, and connections.

Membership is by invitation from a current active member. If you have an invitation code, download the app on iOS and tap Apply to begin. Your application is reviewed by the membership committee and you'll receive an email once there's an update. On rare occasions, exceptional applications without a code are considered — you can submit one on this page.

We work with venues and businesses that don't offer deals to the general public. The process exists to ensure members are a genuine fit for the community and uphold the values of HomeQuarters. Quality over quantity — always.

Gold is the standard HQ membership. It gives you access to exclusive deals and discounts at curated partner venues across the UK, invitations to members-only events, and the ability to connect and discover other members from your community.

Platinum is an optional add-on (£15/month) that includes everything in Gold, plus access to a curated network of top venues back in your home country — restaurants, lounges, and clubs selected for quality. Currently available for Zimbabwe (Harare, Bulawayo, Vic Falls), with more destinations coming soon.

Yes. Platinum renews monthly. You can cancel at any time from the Billing section in the app — there are no cancellation fees and your access continues until the end of the billing period.

Gold membership does not expire as long as your account remains in good standing. Platinum is subscription-based and remains active while your payments are up to date. HomeQuarters reserves the right to suspend or terminate membership in line with the House Rules.

Yes. As an active member you can nominate people you genuinely know and vouch for. Invitations are non-transferable and confidential — your reputation is attached to every nomination you make.

Conduct

HomeQuarters fosters a community of respect, warmth, and trust. Conduct that falls short of these values — including rudeness to venue staff, harassment of other members, abuse of benefits, or anything that damages the reputation of the community — may result in immediate suspension or permanent revocation, regardless of membership tier. Access is a privilege, not a right.
